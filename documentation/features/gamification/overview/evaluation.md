| Lesson | Supporting finding / evidence | Documentation action or disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adding a tab to a fixed-width tab bar is a responsive change, not just a list edit. A four-item bar that fits desktop can push the whole page wider at 375 px, and nothing in the unit or route suites catches it — only a real narrow-viewport measurement does | ACH-01, EV-15, EV-16 | No authority change proposed.
